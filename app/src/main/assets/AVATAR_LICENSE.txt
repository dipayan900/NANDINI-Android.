The model is a stylized anime 3D character, not a photorealistic human and not the proprietary MYRAA character. It is being used as a legally reusable visual alternative while preserving the existing NANDINI VRM animation pipeline.

The Three.js and @pixiv/three-vrm libraries are loaded from jsDelivr at runtime under their respective open-source licenses.
